# impulse (field)

the $\Delta\phi^*$ field of a [[signal]]: the proven shift in [[focus]] the signal carries. cybergraph names the field and gives it its slot in the structure; it routes the complete definition to the component that owns it.

how $\Delta\phi^*$ is computed (locally, on the neuron's neighborhood), what it is worth (self-minted reward), and how it is conserved per epoch — the complete form — is the [[tru]]'s. see [[impulse]] for the full specification.

cybergraph's only role: the field exists in the signal, it is covered by the [[proof]] σ, and a verifier checks that proof. nothing more.
