# staking

directing economic weight toward [[particles]] and [[axons]]. two mechanisms, two homes:

## conviction — per-link

the $(\tau, a)$ a [[cyberlink]] carries is a [[box]] locked into that specific edge. high conviction on one link signals "I bet specifically on this connection." a box can be maintained, withdrawn (spend back to wallet), or transferred (spend to a new owner) — the assertion stays in $L$, the position is liquid. this is cybergraph's: see [[box]] for the full model.

## will — broad

locking [[$CYB]] for a duration produces [[will]], which auto-distributes [[attention]] across every link a [[neuron]] creates. longer lock → more will → more attention per link. will, attention, and how they feed the [[focus]] fixed point are computed by the [[tru]] — see [[will]] and [[tru]].

## the split

conviction prices a single claim (a box on one edge); will prices the neuron's whole portfolio (broad attention). together they give each neuron a range from passive participation to high-conviction betting. the economic input to focus — conviction + will-derived attention, weighted by [[karma]] and the [[inversely coupled bonding surface|ICBS]] market — is assembled and resolved in [[tru]].

see [[box]] for the conviction box model · [[will]] for the lock mechanics · [[tru]] for how staking becomes focus.
